What does the confidence column mean?

Each forecast is the consensus of up to 15 weather models and 5 wave models. The confidence label compares how much those models disagree about this slot:

  • HIGH Models within ~5 kn (wind), ~0.3 m (waves/swell), ~3 hPa (pressure), or ~2 °C of each other.
  • MED Larger spread but still useful — treat the figure as a working estimate.
  • LOW Models disagree significantly. Cross-check against another forecast source before sailing on this slot.

Numbers shown are pessimistic — the worst-case across all surviving models. Wind / waves / swell are ranges (low–high) so you can see how variable the slot is; gusts, pressure, temp, visibility are single values.
